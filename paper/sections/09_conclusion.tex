\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Weight-space programming turns a long-standing metaphor, weights as programs, into a procedure: a language model reads a network and writes the change. In its first three weeks, the field produced reproducible repairs, from-scratch networks that behave like compiled programs, and careful audits that located what the editor actually uses. It also produced cheap backdoors and the first models that edit themselves. The weakest link is not capability but reliability. Cheap alternatives absorb much of the reported gain, reading leans on priors, results vary across sessions, and almost nothing is known about whether edits persist.

These are experimental gaps, not conceptual ones, and the problems in \cref{sec:open} are written so that progress on them can be checked. We will update this survey as the literature develops. The corpus, reading notes and generator scripts are released with each version at \url{https://github.com/Abelo9996/weight-space-programming-survey}.
